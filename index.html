Syllabus Overview

The ISTQB CTFL v4.0.1 syllabus is organized into six chapters. Each chapter contributes a specific weight to the final exam.

Fundamentals of Testing

Testing principles, the test process, the psychology of testing, and ethics. Foundation for all testing activities.

8 questions
2 15%

Testing Throughout the SDLC

Test levels, test types, maintenance testing, and the relationship between development and testing activities.

6 questions

Static analysis, review processes, review types, and the role of reviews in quality assurance.

4 25%

Test Techniques

Black-box, white-box, and experience-based test techniques. Equivalence partitioning, BVA, decision tables, and state transition testing.
